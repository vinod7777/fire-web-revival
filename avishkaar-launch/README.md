# 🚀 Avishkaar Season 4 - Official Launch Ceremony Portal

A dedicated, standalone stage inauguration website designed specifically for the College Director and Dignitaries to inaugurate and launch the official **Avishkaar Season 4** website.

---

## 🌊 Key Highlights & Features

1. **Top-Left Header**:
   - High-definition **AITAM Logo** & **IIC Logo** in a glassmorphic badge.
   - Text credit: *Aditya Institute of Technology and Management (AITAM) & IIC Presents*.

2. **Stage Centerpiece**:
   - Glowing bioluminescent title: **AVISHKAAR** (National Innovation Hackathon).
   - The **AITAM 25 Years Silver Jubilee Logo** with 3D orbital rings and levitation.
   - Grand **LAUNCH AVISHKAAR** button.

3. **Inauguration Sequence (Triggered on Launch Click)**:
   - **Step 1 (0s - 3s)**: The **25 Years Emblem** performs a high-speed 3D hyper-spin with sci-fi power-up sound effects.
   - **Step 2 (3s - 7s)**: Slogan morphing animation:
     - *"Avishkaar is the **BRAND** of AITAM"*
     - *"Avishkaar is the **PRIDE** of AITAM"*
     - *"Avishkaar is the **FEST** of AITAM"*
   - **Step 3 (7s - 12s)**: **Legacy Glimpse Gallery** — 3D floating gallery cards showcasing real photos from previous Avishkaar seasons (Hackathon kickoff, 48-hour coding, mentoring, live demos, and prize ceremony).
   - **Step 4 (12s - 16s)**: Grand Climax reveal of **AVISHKAAR SEASON 4** with confetti cannon explosions, celebratory fanfare, and director inauguration badge.
   - **Step 5 (16s+)**: Auto-redirects to `http://localhost:8080/` (with countdown and immediate override button).

4. **Grand Stage Soundtrack (Two Steps From Hell - "Victory")**:
   - Integrated the official stage launch track: **Two Steps From Hell — Victory**.
   - **Start Cue Selector** in header:
     - `Cue: 0:45 (Choir & Drums Drop)` *(Default)*: Drops immediately with thunderous battle drums & angelic choir when LAUNCH is clicked.
     - `Cue: 0:00 (Melodic Intro)`: Starts with gentle symphonic strings and builds up into the climax.
   - **Soundcheck / Test BGM button**: Allows the stage sound engineer to test auditorium audio levels before the dignitaries arrive.
   - **Climax Volume Swell**: BGM automatically surges to 100% volume with the confetti explosion at the Season 4 reveal.
   - Synchronized with Web Audio low-frequency sub-bass impact booms on each slogan reveal (*BRAND*, *PRIDE*, *FEST*).
   - Top-right **Master Audio ON/Mute** and **Fullscreen (F11)** controls.
